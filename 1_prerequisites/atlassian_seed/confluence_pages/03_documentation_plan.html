Editorial rules

  1. Write for any employee who wants to submit one time-off request.
  2. Do not mention the old email process except when explicitly replacing old content.
  3. Keep steps action-oriented and numbered.
  4. Every step includes a clear outcome ("You see ...").
  5. Use "you". Use active voice and present tense.
  6. If a source is uncertain, mark it as a verification note instead of presenting it as final.
  7. Do not invent policies, deadlines, button labels, menu names, or statuses.
  8. Keep sentences under 25 words.

Review checklist

  • Every factual claim has a source: a Confluence page or a Jira ticket.
  • Screenshots match the final build. Check dates and labels.
  • No retired terms. Check the latest comments on the Jira tickets.
  • A second person reviews before publication.
