Summary

Brightside Co. is moving time-off requests from email to the Employee Portal. This page is the source of truth for what changes and when.

Effective date: 2026-11-01

What changed

  • The Time Off form in the Employee Portal replaces email requests to managers.
  • Find the form at Employee Portal > Time Off > My Requests.
  • Requests of 1 or 2 days are approved automatically if the employee has enough balance.
  • Requests of 3 or more days go to the manager, who has 2 business days to respond.
  • Requests of 3 or more days must be submitted at least 5 business days before the start date.
  • The remaining balance now shows on the Time Off page. Employees no longer check with a manager.
  • An employee can cancel a request in the portal until the day before it starts.

What did not change

  • Vacation and personal days still come from the same balance.
  • Unpaid leave still needs written approval from HR.

Open question

The team has not confirmed whether the portal menu path is final. Verify it before publishing any documentation.
